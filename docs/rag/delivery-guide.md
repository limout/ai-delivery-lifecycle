# Delivery practices (demo)

This is synthetic internal guidance for the AI Delivery Copilot demo. It is not a real customer document.

## Discovery questions

Ask what problem the software solves, who the users are, which systems already exist, and whether there is a hard date. Separate facts the customer stated from assumptions the team is making. Do not invent a budget, a stack, or a timeline.

Useful discovery questions:

- Who are the named user groups?
- Which existing systems must integrate?
- What is in the first release versus later phases?
- Is the date a target or a contractual deadline?

## Risk identification

Late surprises usually come from unnamed integrations, unclear first-release scope, and status reports that stay green while work is blocked. Track risks that affect estimate confidence: missing access, third-party dependencies, and unresolved compliance.

## Stakeholder management

The Head of Delivery needs an honest picture, not a cheerful slide. Internal RAG (red/amber/green) should match what the customer will experience. If information is missing, say it is unknown rather than filling the gap with optimism.
