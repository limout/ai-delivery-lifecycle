# Past projects (synthetic)

Fictional delivery notes for RAG demonstration only. No real clients.

## Project Atlas — employee expense portal

Cause of trouble: the team treated “integrate with finance” as a single story. Finance used two ledgers and a weekly file drop. Outcome: the first release slipped four weeks; a paid discovery would have found the second ledger.

## Project Harbor — customer self-service

Cause of trouble: a two-month target was treated as a promise. Independent estimate was 12–16 weeks. Outcome: the account received a discount after a late red status. Lesson: label deadline feasibility explicitly, including when it is not demonstrated.

## Project Quill — reporting warehouse

Cause of trouble: PMs spent Friday rewriting status from Jira screenshots. The Head of Delivery learned a stream was blocked from the customer, not from the pack. Outcome: a weekly exception list of at most three projects reduced fire drills.
